## Wiring Diagram (Connections)

### LCD Display (I2C Adapter)
| LCD Pin | Arduino Pin |
|---------|-------------|
| SDA     | A4          |
| SCL     | A5          |
| VCC     | 5V          |
| GND     | GND         |

### Ultrasonic Sensor HC‑SR04
| HC‑SR04 Pin | Arduino Pin |
|-------------|-------------|
| VCC         | 5V          |
| GND         | GND         |
| Trig        | 9           |
| Echo        | 10          |

### Buzzer
| Buzzer Pin | Arduino Pin |
|------------|-------------|
| Positive (+) | 11         |
| Negative (-) | GND        |

### IR Receiver
| IR Receiver Pin | Arduino Pin |
|-----------------|-------------|
| VCC             | 5V          |
| GND             | GND         |
| Signal          | 12          |

### PIR Sensor
| PIR Pin | Arduino Pin |
|---------|-------------|
| VCC     | 5V          |
| GND     | GND         |
| OUT     | 8           |
